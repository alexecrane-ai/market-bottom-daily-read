How signals are defined. Fast drop below 25: VIX closes under 25, was at or above 25 within the last 10 sessions, and is down at least 20% from its 10-day high. 90% day: at least 90% of S&P 500 members closed up (or down) versus the prior close. This is a large-cap proxy; the classic Lowry study uses NYSE up-volume and points gained. Junk spreads: ICE BofA US High Yield option-adjusted spread from FRED (usually one business day behind). Volatile = 20-day swing size in the top 20% of the past year, or widening of 30+ bps in 5 days or 50+ bps in 20 days. Calm = swing size below the 50th percentile with little widening. In the VIX and spread tables, red means rising (more stress) and green means falling. Not investment advice.
